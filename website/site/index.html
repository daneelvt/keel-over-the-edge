What waits out there

  • Ride out a gale

    Whitecaps, spray and gusts that lay you over. Ease the sheet in time.

  • Thread the reefs

    Surf breaks white below the cliffs. Read the water before you close the shore.

  • Sight new land

    Each week brings islands no one has seen. Sight one first and the chart carries your name.

  • Find the hidden lagoons

    Waterfalls, villages and quiet coves, tucked inside the islands for those who look.

  • Sail by the stars

    Nights are dark. Find your way by the stars, the lighthouses and your own reckoning.

  • Skim the edge

    Sail close enough to watch the sea pour over. Go too far and you wash up in port.
