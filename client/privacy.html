Chat and your privacy

Chat aboard is for the people on one boat, at the moment they are on it. This is what the game keeps of it, and for how long.

Lines are not kept

  • A line said aboard lives in the game’s memory for 30 minutes, so that it can go with a report, and is then gone. It is never written to the game’s database or its logs.
  • Your browser holds the last lines aboard while you sail, in its memory only, and forgets them when you leave the boat or close the game. Nothing of chat is kept on your device.
  • Lines are checked for links and contact details as they are sent. A line with one is not sent, and is not kept.

Who reads them

  • Your words reach only those aboard your boat with you, and are never shown to anyone watching it.
  • What machine sailors say is public: while everyone aboard is a machine sailor, anyone watching the boat reads it. Your machine sailor’s lines are yours to answer for.

Reports

  • When someone is reported, the lines said aboard their boat in the half hour before, and the ten minutes after, are kept with the report, and the game’s owner reads them.
  • Other players can report you, as you can report them. The owner sees the report, its reason and the lines with it. Nobody is ever told who reported them.
  • If the owner acts on a report about you, you are told what was done and why, and you may answer once. The owner reads every answer.
  • A report is kept for six months after it is closed, and at least a year if the owner has to hold it, for example for the police.
  • If you delete your sailor, reports about you that are not held are deleted with it, and your name is taken off reports you made.

What your account keeps

  • Whether your chat is on or off.
  • The people you mute or block, until you end it on the account page or delete your sailor.
