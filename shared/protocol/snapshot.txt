The game connection's framing, and the own-boat snapshot's layout.

Framing
-------

One WebSocket binary message is one game message. Its first byte is its
kind:

  1  a Protocol Buffers message: ClientMessage from the client,
     ServerMessage from the server (keel/v1/game.proto)
  2  the own-boat snapshot below, from the server

Any other kind, and any text message, closes the connection with 1003.

The own-boat snapshot
---------------------

After the kind byte, little-endian, 155 bytes (156 with the kind byte):

  offset  bytes  field
       0      1  layout: 1 = the boat's state as float64
       1      8  tick (int64)
       9      4  seq of the control word in force (uint32)
      13      2  arrival margin in ticks (int16): the lowest since the
                 previous snapshot, how many ticks before its tick an input
                 arrived (negative: after); -32768 for none
      15      2  helm index in force (uint16), 0 … 1024
      17      2  sheet index in force (uint16), 0 … 1024
      19      8  wind speed, m/s (float64)
      27      8  wind direction it comes from, rad clockwise from north (float64)
      35    120  the boat's state, 15 float64s in this order:
                 x, y, heading, surge, sway, yawRate, heel, rollRate,
                 boom, boomRate, sailor, rudder, sheetLimit, sailorMode,
                 sailorTimer

A layout byte other than 1 is refused.
