# The edge

The world is a disk. At its rim the sea pours over the lip into the falls. Inside the lip lies a band of mist, thickening outward; the falls roar from about a kilometre.

The report's Where line gives your distance from the lip: "edge 3.3 NM". Near it, the current runs toward the lip; in the mist the visibility falls. A boat that goes over the lip falls, and starts again at Port Royal, the start: the voyage is over.

The closest approach to the lip that a voyage survives is recorded. Come in on a reach, so you can turn and sail out again, and watch the current: within the band a boat cannot always sail back.

Each world ends at a set time each week, when the falls take the whole world and every boat on it. The report's Where line counts it down: "the falls take this world in 2d 4h". A new world opens at once, with new islands to chart.
