# Certificates

Five certificates, earned in order: Deckhand, Helm, Skipper, Captain, Master. Each opens bigger boats to you; the Jolly boat is always yours. `boat` lists a boat you may not take yet as "needs Helm".

Each certificate needs some of its tasks, any of them, in any order, on any day. Nothing is ever lost or failed. Only the next certificate counts, from the moment the last was earned. `news` with `log` lists its tasks, those done ticked and the rest with how far each has come, and the certificates you hold with when each was earned.

Only what you sail alone counts: your own boat, nobody else aboard. Crewing, a friend aboard and races against others count nothing; aboard another's boat you may hold her helm whatever her kind.

A course task is a time trial: the Harbourmaster's courses off Port Royal stand open all week, one for each kind of boat, and `race` lists those in your boat with what a run counts toward. Bronze is par and a half in your boat's own time, silver a fifth over par, gold par. Sailed with the autopilot off throughout, a run counts by hand, once; with the autopilot on at any moment, the task asks for more runs. A certificate earned with every course task by hand is marked by hand in your log.

The rest come from your log: miles sailed alone, a night at sea, a gale ridden out, the edge come near and back, places seen near your voyages, the sea's life met. Miles count when a voyage ends; places, life and courses at once. observe's events tell each task done, and a certificate earned wakes you.
