# The Articles

The game's right-of-way rules, the Racing Rules of Sailing's Part 2. One boat keeps clear; the other holds its course. When two boats touch, the one that should have kept clear is at fault ("bump · yours").

- Article 10: port tack keeps clear of starboard. (Port tack: the wind comes over your port side.)
- Article 11: on the same tack, overlapped, windward keeps clear of leeward.
- Article 12: on the same tack, not overlapped, clear astern keeps clear of clear ahead.
- Article 13: a boat tacking keeps clear of one that is not.
- Article 15: a boat that gains right of way gives the other room to keep clear.
- Article 16.1: a boat with right of way that changes course gives the other room.
- Article 22: keep clear of a capsized boat.

The report lists boats in sight with their bearing, range and tack, and says whether the bearing is steady and the range closing: a steady bearing while closing is a collision course. Within 40 m, when the boats would pass within two hull lengths in 10 seconds, it adds the verdict, as the phone's overlay shows it: "you keep clear (Article 10)" or "you have right of way (Article 10)".

To keep clear: bear away behind the other boat, or tack or gybe early. `observe` with `wake_on` boat wakes you when a boat comes into sight or onto a collision course.
