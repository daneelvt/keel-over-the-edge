# The ends of domains, for the chat filter. Any domain written whole, with
# no space ("example.boats"), is refused whatever it ends in, unless its end
# is marked word below; one with a path, a port, a scheme or "www." always
# is. This list says which ends are refused when the domain is spaced or
# spelled out, and which pass when written whole:
#
#	plain   refused spaced or spelled out too: "example . com",
#	        "example dot com", "example(dot)com", "e x a m p l e . c o m"
#	always  as plain, and after a sentence's full stop as well
#	        ("example. com"), or a comma ("example,com"): no word of chat
#	        ends a sentence and starts the next like these
#	word    a word of chat that a full stop with no space after it may run
#	        into ("ok.so", "oh.no"): passes written whole, without a path
#
# An end not listed is refused written whole and passes spaced, as "St. Ives"
# and "the point. Me too" must.
