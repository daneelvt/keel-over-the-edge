# Other apps, where a player could be asked to go: a chat line naming one
# with a contact cue ("add me on", "my snap is", "on discord") is refused.
# Each entry is a name, lowercase letters and digits as the chat filter folds
# them ("snap chat" and "snapchat" are both found as snapchat), then how
# much it takes to refuse it:
#
#	any    a name that is no ordinary word: any cue, even "on" before it
#	       ("on snap"), or "got" or "have" with the name last ("got
#	       insta?")
#	named  a short form, a word that is also a name, or somewhere that is
#	       no way to someone by itself ("I watched it on youtube"): a cue
#	       that names it as a contact ("add me on steam", "my steam id",
#	       "sc me", "my youtube channel")
#	on     an ordinary word at sea: only a message verb and "on" or "via"
#	       ("add me on line", "message me on signal"), or "my line id"
#	mail   a mail service: as named, and "at" or "@" before it
#
# A word sailors use for something else stays out, or is only "on": "the
# line squall", "fog signal", "full steam ahead" and "my snap shackle" pass.
