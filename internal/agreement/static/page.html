Signing under 18

Thank you. A contributor under 18 signs together with a parent or guardian, by e-mail. Nothing has been recorded yet.

  1. Your parent or guardian reads the agreement with you: the whole of it, and the plain-words summary at its top.
  2. They write to {{.Contact}}, saying that they are your parent or guardian, giving their full name and yours, and your GitHub account, @{{.User.Login}}, and that they have read the {{.Version.Name}} with you and consent to you agreeing to it.
  3. The owner replies with the agreement's text, and you both answer "I agree".
  4. The owner records your signature, with your parent's or guardian's name, and your pull requests are checked again at once.

Only your parent's or guardian's name and e-mail are kept with your signature, as the privacy notice says.
